Discover what's happening around you. Therr matches you with nearby friends, local hotspots, and curated social events — then sends you calendar invites so you never miss a moment.

Unlike traditional social media, Therr rewards real-world engagement over passive scrolling. Content activates when you're near where it was posted, creating a treasure-hunt experience that makes exploring your neighborhood feel new again.                                           

  How It Works:

  1. Set your preferences — group size, event type, distance
  2. Get matched with friends (and friends-of-friends) who share your interests
  3. Receive curated activity ideas and calendar invites
  4. Show up, check in, and earn rewards

  Key Features:

  - Proximity-based discovery — Find posts, deals, and hidden gems that only unlock when you're nearby
  - Automated social planning — Opt in to recurring matches with curated local activities
  - Rewards for real engagement — Earn rewards for check-ins, reviews, and supporting local businesses
  - Local business promotions — Explore geo-fenced deals and discover new spots in your area
  - Safe by design — Multi-factor authentication, explicit content filters, no selling user data to third parties
  - No toxic algorithms — A healthier feedback loop built for authentic connection
