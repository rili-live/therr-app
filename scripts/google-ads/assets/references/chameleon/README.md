# Chameleon — character reference

Reference still for the **"Evolve your life"** promo video (Friends with Habits) — the
chameleon who sparks the caveman's change of habit and delivers the closing line,
*"Friends with Habits. Evolve your life."* See [`../caveman/`](../caveman/) for the other
character.

**This is a reference image, not an ad asset.** Do not upload it to Google Ads or Meta.
It exists to keep the chameleon consistent across separately generated video shots — feed
it into Runway's References / image-to-video as the character source.

| File | Look |
|---|---|
| `chameleon-a-purple-branch.png` | Violet-purple, tall helmet casque with beaded edge, large amber eyes, coiled spiral tail; three-quarter, perched on a thin branch; soft green bokeh |

2048×2048, generated with Seedream 5.0 Pro from the prompt beginning *"A vertical 9:16
portrait of a small chameleon with a distinctive helmet-shaped hea[d]…"*. The prompt asked
for 9:16 but the render is square; that does not matter for a reference.

Character text to paste into each shot prompt alongside the reference:

> a small chameleon with a tall helmet-shaped head crest with a beaded edge, large
> expressive amber eyes, a tightly coiled spiral tail and rich violet-purple skin

The colour shift in the "spark" shot (violet → teal → lime → gold) echoes the in-app
daily-bonus chameleon reveal, so the ad and the app share a beat.
