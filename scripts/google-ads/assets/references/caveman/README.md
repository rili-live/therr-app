# Caveman — character reference

Reference stills for the **"Evolve your life"** promo video (Friends with Habits): a
caveman repeats the same failed attempt at making fire until a chameleon sparks a change
of habit, and he finally gets a flame. The chameleon then turns to camera: *"Friends with
Habits. Evolve your life."*

**These are reference images, not ad assets.** Do not upload them to Google Ads or Meta.
They exist to keep the caveman consistent across separately generated video shots — feed
one into Runway's References / image-to-video as the character source.

| File | Look |
|---|---|
| `caveman-a-long-hair-sand.png` | Long, straight reddish-brown hair; near-front three-quarter; sandy ground, warm haze |
| `caveman-b-curly-hair-rock.png` | Curly dark-brown hair; three-quarter; rocky outcrop, grey haze |

Both are 1024×1024, generated with Seedream 5.0 Pro from the prompt beginning *"A
full-body portrait of a stocky, good-natured caveman standing in three-quarter…"*. They
share the build, fur tunic and vine belt but **not the hair** — pick one as the canonical
caveman and use only that one for every shot, or the character will drift between clips.

Character text to paste into each shot prompt alongside the reference:

> a stocky, good-natured caveman with a wild mop of dark-brown hair, a bushy beard, thick
> expressive eyebrows, a tan fur tunic tied with a vine belt, bare feet

Style is stylized 3D animated feature-film, deliberately not live-action, so the character
reads as an original cartoon rather than any existing ad campaign's cavemen.
