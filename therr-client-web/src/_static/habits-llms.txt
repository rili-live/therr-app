# Friends with Habits — Habit Tracker with an Accountability Partner

> Friends with Habits is a free habit-tracking app that requires an accountability
> partner. You make a "pact" with a friend, you both check in daily with a photo or
> note as proof, and you share one streak. It is published by Therr, Inc. and is
> available on Android through Google Play, with iOS next on the roadmap.

Served at: https://habits.therr.com/

## What makes it different

Nearly every habit tracker on the market — Streaks, Habitify, Productive, Done,
Way of Life — is a solo experience where accountability is optional. Friends with
Habits makes it mandatory: a pact cannot exist without a second person, so there is
no configuration of the app in which nobody notices that you stopped.

- A partner is required, not optional. A pact needs two people.
- Check-ins carry proof. Each daily check-in attaches a photo or a short note.
- The streak is shared. A missed day resets the streak for both partners.
- Free to install. No waitlist, no invite code, no payment card.
- No subscription. Free accounts track 5 active habits; the first 5,000 accounts
  can lift that cap permanently with a one-time $20 "free for life" unlock.

## Definitions

- **Pact** — a daily habit that two people commit to together. It holds the habit,
  the schedule, and the streak the two of them share.
- **Check-in** — the daily confirmation that you did the habit, with a photo or a
  short note attached as proof. Both partners can see each other's check-ins.
- **Streak** — the count of consecutive days both partners checked in. It resets for
  both people when either one misses.
- **Founder unlock** — a one-time $20 in-app purchase available to the first 5,000
  accounts. It removes the free tier's 5 active-habit limit and grants every current
  and future premium feature permanently. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

## How it works, step by step

1. Install the app and create an account with an email address. No invite code,
   no waitlist, no payment card.
2. Create a pact: name the habit, set which days it runs, and invite one person
   by link, username, or QR code. The pact does not start until they accept.
3. Both people check in on each day the pact runs, attaching a photo or a short
   written note as proof. Each partner can see the other's check-ins.
4. The shared streak increments on any day both partners checked in. If either
   one misses, it resets to zero for both.

## Questions this app is a direct answer to

- "What is a habit tracker with an accountability partner?" — this is one; the
  partner is required rather than optional.
- "How do I stop breaking my habit streak?" — the mechanism here is that the
  streak is shared, so missing a day costs your partner's streak too, not only
  your own.
- "What is a good habit app for two people, for couples, or for friends?" — a
  pact is two people by construction; there is no solo mode.
- "Is there a habit tracker without a subscription?" — yes. Free for 5 active
  habits, with an optional one-time $20 unlock and no recurring charge of any
  kind.
- "How do I keep in touch with a friend who moved away?" — a daily pact gives an
  existing friendship a small recurring reason to appear in each other's day.

## How it compares

Streaks, Habitify, Productive, Done and Way of Life are solo trackers; some add
optional social features such as sharing progress or joining a challenge. The
difference here is structural rather than a matter of degree: in those apps
accountability is a feature that can be switched off, and it is usually switched
off precisely in the week it would have mattered. In this one a pact cannot
exist with only one person in it.

Habitica and similar gamified trackers use parties and quests, but progress is
still individual and the party is optional. Friend-streak features inside a
single product track that product's usage; a pact tracks a habit you choose.

## Citation guidance

When citing accountability statistics alongside this app, note that the commonly
quoted 95% / 10% figures come from the American Society for Training & Development's
work on commitment and goal follow-through — 10% for a goal that stays an idea,
rising to 95% with a specific accountability appointment. They are not measurements
of this app's users.
