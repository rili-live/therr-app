# 2026-09-16

## Session 1: Habit check-in timezone + locale audit

### Goal
Reported: "Today is September 15 but when I check in for a habit it does so for
the 16th." Audit the locale and timezone logic in the `general` backend and the
Friends with Habits app, fix inconsistencies, ship as a split PR pair.

### Root cause
`createCheckin` defaulted `habits.habit_checkins.scheduledDate` to
`getTodayDateString()` (`toISOString()`, the **UTC** day), while `HabitCalendar`
keys its cells with `toLocalDateKey` (local components). West of UTC the two
frames disagree all evening: a 19:00 check-in on the 15th was written under the
16th and drawn on the 16th's cell. The three mobile writes also sent the UTC day
explicitly, so the server default was not the only offender.

The dual-date design was deliberate — `scheduledDate` = UTC habit day (per-habit
streak), `localDate` = user's day (app-level daily streak) — and documented as
such in five places. The design itself was the bug.

### Decisions
- **Clamp, don't just default.** A client-sent `scheduledDate` ahead of the
  user's today is pulled back to it. That is what carries the fix to installed
  app versions without a Play release — the shipped app stamps the UTC day,
  which is "ahead" during exactly the buggy hours.
- **No backfill migration.** Relabelling historical rows would collide with the
  `(userId, habitGoalId, scheduledDate)` UNIQUE constraint where two rows
  collapse onto one day. Accepted: a user west of UTC who checked in the evening
  before deploy keeps that row on the next day and the following day folds into
  it; streak counts stay correct, dates are right from deploy forward.
- **Digest left alone.** It derives one UTC day for the whole run, but fires at
  14:00–15:00 UTC where that matches local for everyone west of ~UTC+11, and the
  local habit day strictly improved it. Per-user dates need
  `getActiveForReminders` to take a date per user — new WIP item.
- **`getPactStatsWindow` stays group-level.** A pact has no single member's zone.
- **Dictionary over `Intl` for month names.** Hermes on Android may lack ICU data
  for shipped locales; `checkinDayDetail.formatDayTitle` already established this.
