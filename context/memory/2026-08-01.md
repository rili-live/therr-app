## Design decisions
- Each rung gates on a *different kind* of evidence, so volume alone can't reach the top.
- Mindset requires a **comeback** — an uninterrupted habit has never been tested.
- Identity requires a **partner affirmation** — the one thing a mandatory-partner app has
  that solo trackers don't. Server enforces a shared active pact; cannot be self-issued.
- Everything ratchets: `votesCast` never resets, `applyStage` refuses to lower a stage.
  Dormancy is display-only.
- Check-in hook is fire-and-forget behind the existing first-completion-for-date guard.

## Gotcha worth remembering
`general` was 29 commits ahead of `niche/HABITS-general`, and one of them
(`ee364b86`) added `therr/require-idempotent-migration` with a **20260730000000**
cutoff. Migrations authored off a stale niche branch but dated after the cutoff are
in scope and will fail lint. Fix is a `hasTable` probe, never
`createTableIfNotExists`. Check `eslint-config/migration-idempotency-cutoff.js`
before writing a migration on a niche branch.
