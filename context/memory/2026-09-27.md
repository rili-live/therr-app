## Goalify competitive research (branch claude/goalify-feature-research-c2ocvm)
- Goal: find which features from Goalify (com.onebytezero.Goalify) are worth building in FwH. Prompted by a Reddit ask for an app that holds your money until you finish a goal.
- Finding: Goalify does NOT hold money or take stakes. It is a habit tracker with join-by-link challenges and a live ranking, quantity goals (such as 180 min/week), group chat, nudges, and a coach/B2B tier (Goalify Pro).
- Ranked gaps: (1) join-by-link group challenges with an in-challenge ranking; (2) generic quantity goals that generalize the savings numeric infra; (3) pact group thread; (4) enforce the existing pact consequences on the honor system before any real money; (5) coach mode (watch only).
- Stakes decision input: use charge-on-failure to a charity via Stripe. Do not hold money in escrow (custody/MTL risk; card holds expire in about 7 days). Do not build winners-split-pot, which is Play's real-money gambling policy. The brief's Phase 2 "cash stakes (winners split pot)" needs a rework.
- Open: no code changed, and no issues filed.
