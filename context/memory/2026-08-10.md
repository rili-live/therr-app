## Decisions
- Brand defaults to HABITS when `x-brand-variation` is absent. The queue column has no
  default and `claimDue` only looks for known BrandVariations values, so a row under `''`
  would sit pending forever.
- `pactExpiring` keyed on the date, not `daysRemaining` — runs either side of midnight
  would otherwise compute different values for the same calendar day.
- `partnerMissedDay` keyed on the member who *slipped*, not the recipient (recipient is
  already half the constraint). Without it, a pact with two slipping partners tells the
  third member about only one.
